# Scheme Information Document (extract)

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT created for testing PortfolioLens. It is not an offer document and describes no real scheme.

Name of the Scheme: Synthetic Core Equity Fund
Type of scheme: An open ended equity scheme predominantly investing in large cap stocks.
Benchmark: NIFTY 100 TRI

## Investment objective

The investment objective of the Scheme is to generate long-term capital appreciation. There is no assurance that the investment objective of the Scheme will be achieved.

## Asset allocation

| Instrument | Indicative allocation (% of net assets) |
|---|---|
| Equity and equity related instruments of large cap companies | 80 to 100 |
| Other equity and equity related instruments | 0 to 20 |
| Debt and money market instruments | 0 to 20 |

## Investment strategy

The fund manager follows a bottom-up approach to stock selection and holds cash and money market instruments for liquidity. As an internal investment restriction, the Scheme will invest at least 85% of its net assets in equity and equity related instruments of large cap companies.
